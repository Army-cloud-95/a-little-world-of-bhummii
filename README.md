# 💗 A Little World of Bhummii ♡

A dreamy, heartfelt, Pinterest-inspired digital scrapbook and birthday surprise experience crafted especially for **Bhumika Tyagi (Bhummii)** on her 21st November birthday!

---

## 🌸 What's Inside

1. **The Surprise Landing Hero**:
   - Dreamy typography, floating sparkles, stars, and petals.
   - Live date badge (`21 November`).
   - "Open Your Little Surprise ♡" smooth animated entrance.
2. **Our Little Universe (Photo Gallery)**:
   - Polaroid-style cards with authentic paper textures, washi tape pins, and tilted rotations.
   - Click-to-expand accessible modal lightbox with zoom and next/previous keyboard navigation (`Esc`, `←`, `→`).
3. **Our Memories in Motion (Video Gallery)**:
   - Cinematic memory cards with custom play buttons and durations.
   - Fullscreen-capable modal video player.
4. **A Little Timeline of Us**:
   - Dotted friendship ribbon timeline featuring chapters from the day we met to today and beyond.
5. **A Letter for Bhummii (Vintage Sealed Envelope)**:
   - 3D wax seal stamped with initial **"B"**.
   - Interactive envelope opening with paper rustle sound effect.
   - Unfolds into aged vintage parchment with burned edges, stamps, and handwritten cursive calligraphy.
6. **A Birthday Bouquet for Bhummii (Interactive Floral Studio)**:
   - Interactive flower selection with symbolic meanings (Pink Roses, Tulips, Sunflowers, Lavender, Daisies, Baby's Breath).
   - Wrapped in rustic craft paper with a silk pink ribbon and gift tag: *"For Bhummii ♡"*.
   - Blossom shower confetti and chime when presented!
7. **Make a Wish, Bhummii! (Interactive Birthday Cake)**:
   - 3D tiered birthday cake with strawberry frosting.
   - Individual clickable candles (blow out with smoke effect) or "Blow All Candles" button.
   - Fullscreen celebration confetti cannon and sound effects!
8. **Reasons Why You're My Favourite Person**:
   - Scrapbook cards for: *Your smile*, *Your craziness*, *Our endless conversations*, *Our inside jokes*, and *All the little things*.
   - **"One More Surprise? ♡"** button that reveals sweet, randomized bestie messages with particle bursts.
9. **Final Birthday Screen**:
   - Cinematic closing wish, photobooth strip collage, and signature footer.
10. **Dreamy Music Box Soundtrack**:
    - Built-in zero-dependency Web Audio API soothing lullaby music box with visible toggle button in the navbar.

---

## 📁 How to Add Your Personal Photos & Videos

All memories are centrally managed so you never have to touch complicated code:

1. **Add Photos**:
   - Place your photos inside `public/images/` (e.g. `photo1.jpg`, `photo2.jpg`, `photo3.jpg`).
2. **Add Videos**:
   - Place your video files inside `public/videos/` (e.g. `memory1.mp4`, `memory2.mp4`).
3. **Customize Details**:
   - Open `src/data/memories.js`.
   - Update captions, dates, story chapters, or the letter text anytime!
